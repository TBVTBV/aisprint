The bundle we worked from: fed whole, not from a blank prompt

Per the Session 2 run sheet, ideation was given insights, HMW, the lean lens, our own ideas already on the table, and the ethics guardrails together.

InsightsI1 route not knowledge · I2 verification is the visit · I3 holes sit on organisational boundaries · I4 instructional and reciprocal, not motivational · I5 confidence without accuracy
POVA pediatric pulmonologist facing a new patient needs to reach and verify the raw pieces of that child's history faster, not to be handed a summary, because the visit is consumed by re-assembling a record fragmented at organisational boundaries that is never complete.
How Might WeHMW1 one route, never skip the check · HMW2 ask only for what the record cannot hold, with a safe “I don't know” · HMW3 make the contribution visibly change the room

Diverge: twenty-two concepts, un-edited

Generated wide, including the ones that will not survive. Three were kept deliberately weird because they move the thinking. Selecting a direction below recedes everything it did not absorb, without hiding it.

Show a direction Held back = generated but not carried into A, B or C.
Killed on the way, and why · kept visible so we do not regenerate them
  • “AI writes the visit summary from the chart.” Violates I2 outright. Both physicians said they will not accept another reader's conclusion; one said even his own automation fantasy must remain deletable and editable.
  • “A comprehensive family questionnaire covering the full history.” The clinic already ran a generic version and it lapsed; published completion for electronic pre-visit questionnaires is around half, and some intervention visits ran 6.8 minutes longer.
  • “Integrate with the national exchange to solve fragmentation.” The exchange already carries what it can; the gap is by definition what sits outside it, and this is not buildable inside a three-week sprint.

Converge: three directions, each through the lean lens

One sentence, riskiest assumption, smallest test, and the failure condition decided before the test is run. Every test is runnable this week with no code.

Where we land: the recommendation, and the one thing to run first

ATake as the strongest

A carries the most real evidence, was described unprompted by a participant, sits inside the physician constraint rather than fighting it, and is testable with a paper mock this week. It is also the direction a low-fidelity prototype can genuinely represent: a clickable rubric view is exactly the kind of thing that stands up in a demo without any real system access, which is the scope guidance both mentors gave us.

BCarry alongside, not instead

A alone leaves the second axis untouched: the parent's account, which the physicians said complements and sometimes contradicts the record, and which is the one thing they cannot retrieve from any system. B is where the family half lives, and B's test is a phone call, so it costs five conversations rather than a build. It is also, bluntly, the direction with the weakest evidence underneath it, because everything we hold on families is synthetic, which makes running its test the highest-value thing we can do this week.

CRun in parallel, nearly free

C is one afternoon with the nurse and no build at all. If it works it is a real, shippable improvement; if it fails we learn that the clinic is capacity-bound, which reshapes A and B too.

The one assumption and the one test, for the share-out

Assumption. Physicians will use a consolidated one-route view instead of falling back to the source systems they already trust.

Test. A paper mock of one de-identified case, three physicians, each preparing that patient as they normally would, and we count every time they reach past the mock. If two of three do, we were wrong.
